ℹ️ CelesteCon RPG Guide

How to Play:

  • Movement: Use W A S D or Arrow Keys to move your character around the campus.
  • Fast Travel: Press M to open the sector console, then select OAT Amphitheater, Main Auditorium, or Management HQ.
  • Interaction: Walk close to any character or booth. When the [E] prompt appears, press E to open dialogue.
  • Role Gameplay: Organize events, compete in challenges, or judge debates based on your chosen POV.
  • Minigames: When NPCs face technical failures, launch minigames (CubeSat, Flight Sim, Circuit Patching) to solve their crises!
  • Grand Finale: When you are ready to conclude your festival experience, click the 🏆 AWARDS FINALE button to attend the closing ceremony!
